# Scientific disclaimer

GENOMA is an experimental structural analysis and visualization system.

The following limits are part of the product, not footnotes:

- π is not being claimed to contain hidden information about files.
- The π-derived transformation is deterministic for a given generator version, π offset, and configuration.
- Digital DNA is a structural representation of measured byte statistics.
- Digital DNA is not a cryptographic hash. It is not a substitute for SHA-256 or similar primitives.
- GENOMA does not claim collision resistance.
- Similarity scores are heuristic/statistical unless a future version proves otherwise.
- Anomaly detection is probabilistic/statistical (local deviation, neighbor comparison, structural distance).
- Visualization mappings are designed to make structural properties visible. They are encodings, not discoveries of a biological analogue inside the file.

Do not use GENOMA as evidence of cryptographic uniqueness, malware verdicts, or hidden meaning in π.
